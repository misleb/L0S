# 0013: Entries that scroll, select, copy and paste

Track: Toolkit · Status: blocked · Needs: 0012 · Spec: toolkit.md

## Why

An entry doesn't scroll its text, which is cut short with `...`, and has
no selection, copying or pasting ([toolkit.md, Limits](../spec/toolkit.md#limits)).
Shift-selection and Ctrl-C/Ctrl-V need 0012's modifiers.

## Done when

- An entry scrolls to keep its cursor in view.
- Text can be selected with the pointer and with Shift, and copied and
  pasted between entries.
